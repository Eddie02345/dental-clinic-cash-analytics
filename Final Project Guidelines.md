**What Should the Actual Project Look Like?** 

<u>Here are some examples of projects that would satisfy your guidelines.</u> 

**Project 1: Market Basket Analysis and Product Recommendation System** 

**Client** : Local grocery store or MSME. 

**Problem** : The business does not know which products are commonly purchased together. **Data Mining** : Association Rule Mining (Apriori). 

**Expected Output** : Product associations, purchasing patterns, and promotional recommendations. 

**Project 2: Community Complaint Pattern Analysis System** 

**Client:** Barangay or LGU. 

**Problem:** The barangay has difficulty identifying recurring community complaints and their locations. 

**Data Mining:** Clustering. 

**Expected Output:** Complaint clusters, recurring issues, and areas requiring attention. 

**Project 3: Agricultural Crop Yield Prediction System** 

**Client:** Agricultural cooperative or farmers' association. 

**Problem:** Farmers have difficulty estimating crop production based on historical agricultural data. 

**Data Mining:** Regression or prediction. 

**Expected Output:** Estimated crop yield and factors associated with production. 

**Project 4: Customer Purchasing Behavior Analysis System** 

**Client:** Restaurant or food business. 

**Problem:** The owner has limited knowledge of customer purchasing behavior and preferences. **Data Mining:** Clustering. 

**Expected Output:** Customer segments, purchasing patterns, and business recommendations. 

**Project 5: Community Health Trend Analysis System** 

**Client:** Barangay health center or municipal health office. 

**Problem:** Health personnel need to identify trends in historical, appropriately anonymized health records. **Data Mining:** Classification or clustering. **Expected Output:** Disease occurrence patterns and population-level health trends.
